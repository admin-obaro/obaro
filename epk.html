synopsis

dear rhea, is an 8-track visual album by Nigerian artist obaro. The project explores themes of psychological isolation, longing, and emotional resolution through minimal sound design paired with direct lyric-video narratives.

Designed for high-impact visual listening, each song advances a two-scene storyline, framing personal vulnerability against stark, clinical production.
